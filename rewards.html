        How Accaza Rewards works

        1. Register free with your name and mobile number. We send a one-time code to confirm the number is yours.
        2. Your Rewards badge appears on this page—no app download is required. Show it before paying at the counter.
        3. Eligible completed purchases earn stamps. Your stamp wallet and ready-to-use rewards appear here.
        4. When you redeem, staff scan your live badge or send a code to your registered number. Cancelled claims are safely returned.

        How Dragon Brew Quest works

        Dragon Brew Quest is an optional, limited-time game for Accaza Rewards members. Each season tells a new story, and every mission is a chapter you complete through real café visits.

        1. Tap Accept the Dragon’s Call to enter the current season as a Brew Keeper. Mission 1 unlocks first.
        2. Read the active mission before ordering. It may ask you to explore different drinks, pair a drink with food, collect a quantity, or return on different days.
        3. Show your Rewards badge before payment. Only completed in-store purchases count, and only the active mission receives progress.
        4. Complete the mission to reveal the next chapter. If that chapter has a reward, it appears in your Rewards wallet for staff to redeem.
        5. Returned, voided or corrected qualifying purchases may place the quest reward under review so progress and giveaways stay fair.
