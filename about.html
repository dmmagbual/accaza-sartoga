Our Story

Brewed With Passion

A hidden gem along Governor's Drive — surprisingly spacious, calm and welcoming.

Nestled in the heart of Dasmariñas City, Accaza Coffee House sources the finest beans, roasts with care, and brews with passion. From the roadside near SM Dasmariñas you might not expect it — but step inside and you'll find a surprisingly spacious, serene space that feels worlds away from the bustle outside.

We built Accaza to be a productive sanctuary — a place where students, professionals and friends can focus, study, work, or simply slow down over a well-crafted cup. Every drink, from our signature Sea Salt Caramel series to a classic Spanish Latte, is made with genuine care.

What we promise today

Our Mission

To create a place where every person feels genuinely welcomed, valued, and at home. We serve thoughtfully crafted coffee and food with dependable quality, heartfelt Filipino hospitality, and responsible care for our customers, our team, our community, and the resources entrusted to us.

V
The future we are building

Our Vision

To become one of the Philippines’ most trusted homegrown coffee-house brands—a nationwide presence that nurtures human connection, creates dignified opportunities, and gives every community a true sense of belonging, while growing responsibly without losing the character and purpose at its heart.

Our Core Values

The character we carry into every community

These principles shape how we serve, decide, lead, and grow.

Genuine Hospitality

We welcome every person with warmth, kindness, and respect. Everyone who enters Accaza should feel seen, valued, and sincerely cared for.

Craft with Purpose

We approach every product and every experience with intention. From preparation to presentation, we believe excellence is created through care for the smallest details.

Consistency Builds Trust

We uphold dependable standards in quality, service, cleanliness, and customer care. People should be able to trust the Accaza experience wherever they meet us.

Community and Belonging

We create spaces where people can connect, work, study, celebrate, or simply pause. Accaza exists to bring people together and become a meaningful part of everyday life.

Integrity and Accountability

We choose what is honest and responsible, even when no one is watching. We own our decisions, learn from our mistakes, and protect the trust placed in us.

Growing People Together

We invest in our people, encourage continuous learning, and create dignified opportunities for personal and professional growth. Accaza succeeds when the people behind it flourish.
